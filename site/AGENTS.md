# Site contributor guidance

Preserve the Autonomous Runbook design in `DESIGN.md`. The open-source agent remains the product foundation, but the public conversion journey should focus on hosted continuity without a competing local-install CTA. Do not invent customer counts, placement claims, or application success rates. Keep founding payment copy honest: Hosted and Cloud are both COMING SOON; $49 reserves founding pricing for hosted when it launches — not instant access — and we email a reservation confirmation after verified payment (no instant self-serve dashboard claim unless that path ships). Do not use OPEN or READY on Hosted. Hosted alone shows the founding-reservation CTA; Cloud stays quieter with no reserve affordance. Do not promise launch dates, activation deadlines, or automatic refunds in public offer, FAQ, terms, privacy, or return copy.
